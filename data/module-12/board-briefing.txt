KESTREL FALLS COMMUNITY HOSPITAL: BOARD BRIEFING FOR THE EHR RECOMMENDATION
From Laura Hemmingsen, Chief of Staff (fictional; April 2027)

THE MEETING
- Board of Trustees, Thursday, May 13, 2027, 6:00 p.m.
- Your time: 20 minutes. Plan about 12 minutes to present and 8 minutes for questions.
- Your deck: 8 to 10 slides. Put details the board might ask about in an appendix (appendix slides don't count toward the 10).
- The board packet goes out one week ahead, so the slides must make sense without you in the room.

WHO IS ON THE BOARD (11 members)
- The chair, a retired bank president; a family physician from Prairie Bend; a farm co-op manager; the school superintendent; a retired nurse; a small-business owner; a pastor; two county residents who are patients here; the hospital president (Laura's boss, who doesn't vote); and the chief of the medical staff.
- Most are not health IT experts. Several read every number. The retired nurse will ask about night shift.

WHAT THEY ASKED TO HEAR ABOUT (sent by the chair)
1. What are we being asked to approve, and what does it cost?
2. How will patients be kept safe on go-live day and the weeks after?
3. Will our staff stay with us through this?
4. How will we know in the first 90 days whether it's working?
5. What happens if it goes wrong?

WHAT I NEED FROM YOU
- A recommendation the board can approve or reject, with the decisions spelled out.
- A measures dashboard plan: the 8 measures the board will see after go-live, with targets, warning levels, owners, and how often.

- Honest answers to the chair's five questions.
