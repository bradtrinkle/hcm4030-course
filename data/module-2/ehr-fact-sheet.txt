Kestrel Falls EHR fact sheet (fictional, from Doug Thielen, CIO, and Greg Oltmanns, CFO)
- In use since: 2012 (15 years)
- Users: About 1,900 staff and providers in the hospital, 10 clinics, and the ambulance service
- Patient records: About 410,000
- Interfaces to other systems: 38 (lab, imaging, pharmacy, revenue cycle, ambulance, state immunization registry, regional health information exchange, and others)
- Hospital operating budget: about $375 million a year (EHR cost is about 1.8% of it)
- Total annual EHR cost: $6.8 million (license, support, hosting, and the EHR team)
- Annual Plexmoor support fee (part of the $6.8 million): $2.2 million
- EHR analyst team: 9 people
- Doug's estimate: time to replace an EHR at a hospital our size: 12 to 24 months from signing a contract to go-live (an estimate, not a promise)
