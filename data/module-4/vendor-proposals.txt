Kestrel Falls EHR replacement: vendor proposal summaries (fictional). Dollar figures are 5-year totals unless noted.

Orivane Health
Hosting: Cloud software as a service (Orivane runs it in its cloud)
Time from signing to go-live: 18 months
Clinician usability score (fictional survey): 4.0 out of 5
Reference sites: 3 hospitals of 250 to 400 beds
Price quote, 5 years:
- Subscription (year 1 price x 5 years): $35.25 million
- Implementation: $7.20 million
- Training: $1.65 million
- Interface build: $1.50 million
- Quoted 5-year total: $45.60 million
Notes:
- Ambulance charting connects through a partner product; partner fee is not included in this quote.
- Migrates 7 years of clinical history; older records go to a read-only archive included in the subscription.
Contract excerpts:
- Term: 5 years; renews for 1 year at a time unless either side gives 6 months' notice.
- Price: subscription rises 5% each year starting in year 2 (Section 6.3).
- Uptime: 99.9% each month, with service credits when missed.
- Your data: exported in standard formats at no charge within 90 days after the contract ends.
- Liability cap: 12 months of fees.
- HIPAA business associate agreement: included and signed with the contract.
- Partner fee for ambulance charting integration: $255,000 per year (Appendix C).

Tessarow EHR
Hosting: Hosted in Tessarow's own data center (private cloud)
Time from signing to go-live: 22 months
Clinician usability score (fictional survey): 4.4 out of 5
Reference sites: 2 hospitals of similar size and 4 larger systems
Price quote, 5 years:
- License and hosting (flat, $6.6 million per year x 5): $33.00 million
- Implementation: $6.90 million
- Training: $1.80 million
- Interface build: $1.20 million
- Quoted 5-year total: $42.90 million
Notes:
- Ambulance charting integration included.
- Migrates 2 years of clinical history; older records need a separate archive license ($240,000 per year, not in this quote).
- Early-signing discount: 6% off implementation if signed by February 19, 2027.
Contract excerpts:
- Term: 5 years; price locked for all 5 years.
- Uptime: 99.5% each month; no service credits.
- Your data: exported in standard formats; $45,000 flat fee at exit.
- Liability cap: 12 months of fees.
- HIPAA business associate agreement: included and signed with the contract.

Meridune Health Systems
Hosting: Installed on the hospital's own servers (on premises)
Time from signing to go-live: 15 months
Clinician usability score (fictional survey): 3.6 out of 5
Reference sites: 5 hospitals, all under 150 beds
Price quote, 5 years:
- Software license (one time): $18.00 million
- Annual maintenance ($2.7 million x 5): $13.50 million
- Implementation: $5.70 million
- Training: $1.50 million
- Quoted 5-year total: $38.70 million
Notes:
- Ambulance charting integration included.
- Hospital buys its own servers and storage (Meridune estimate: $3.45 million, not in this quote).
- Meridune recommends the hospital add 6 IT staff to run the system (about $145,000 per person per year in salary plus benefits, not in this quote).
- Migrates all clinical history.
Contract excerpts:
- Term: 5 years; renews automatically for another 5 years unless the hospital gives 12 months' notice.
- Ending early: fee of 50% of the remaining maintenance fees.
- Your data: exported only in Meridune's own format; conversion to standard formats billed at $250 per hour.
- Liability cap: 3 months of fees.
- HIPAA business associate agreement: "available on request."

Limits from Greg and Doug:
- Budget (Greg): no more than $47.0 million over 5 years for everything: the new EHR, any extra staff or hardware, and any Extended Support we must buy from Plexmoor ($3.3 million for 2029).
- Timing (Doug): contract signed by about April 1, 2027. Go-live times in the proposals count from signing. Plexmoor standard support ends December 31, 2028; its regulatory updates end June 30, 2028.
- IT staffing (Doug): IT cannot add staff this year without a new budget request.
