Module 3 rubric: The Transition Team

100 points. Your work is graded on reasoning, not on matching one right answer. Copy this whole page into your coach chat in Step 6.

CriterionPointsFull marksPartialLow
1. Reasoning and judgment (Step 5 team charter and reply)4036 to 40: at least 8 seats that cover every required role and the nights, clinic, and ambulance voices; every person and hour matches the roster, no one gets more hours than approved, and the total is 45 or fewer, shown; each "why" names a skill or perspective the project needs; decision rules, a meeting plan that night and field staff can use, and a real way for at least two left-out groups to be heard; the reply is respectful, explains the decision, and offers a real way to contribute.24 to 35: the team mostly fits, but a required voice is missing, hours are off, or reasons and inclusion plans are thin.0 to 23: over the limits, people not on the roster, missing parts, or no reasons.
2. Feedback record (full coach conversation, what changed and why)1514 to 15: full conversation included; changes clearly tied to specific feedback, with reasons for anything kept or rejected.8 to 13: conversation included but changes are vague or not tied to feedback.0 to 7: conversation missing, cut down to a summary, or no revision.
3. Ethics exercise (ACHE Code, Section IV)1514 to 15: each of the six AI recommendations gets a sound agree-or-change decision checked against the roster and limits; the selection criteria are fair, clear, and usable; the note to Greg ties clearly to items 4 and 7.8 to 13: most recommendations handled, but an unfair exclusion survives or the link to the Code is loose.0 to 7: parts missing, the AI list is accepted as is, or no link to the Code.
4. Copilot use with verification (Step 4)1514 to 15: your own prompt attaches the roster and limits Copilot to it; you check every person it names against the roster and name what it missed or got wrong; the improved prompt fixes a real problem.8 to 13: checking covers only some names or is general ("it looked right").0 to 7: no real prompt, no file attached, no checking, or a copied prompt.

6. Reflection109 to 10: compares Step 3 with the final charter, names specific changes and why, and connects to a real decision a manager makes about who is in the room.5 to 8: general reflection.0 to 4: missing or off topic.

Not graded on its own: Step 3 (first call without AI). It is your starting point for the reflection. Choices A and B in Step 5, Part 3, are graded the same way.
