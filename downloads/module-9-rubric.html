Module 9 rubric: Step 6, Challenges

100 points. Your work is graded on reasoning, not on matching one right answer. Copy this whole page into your coach chat in Step 6.

CriterionPointsFull marksPartialLow
1. Reasoning and judgment (Step 5 risk register and summary)4036 to 40: all ten risks scored with likelihood and impact, the arithmetic right, and the evidence named from the memos or measures; each owner is one named person who can act; responses fit the risk (avoid, reduce, transfer, or accept) and are specific; warning signs move early and have a trigger level; X1 is a real risk the memos missed; the top three are ranked with at least two trends from the measures file; the money answer protects power and cooling and sizes the cash cushion with arithmetic; the accepted risk has a real backup plan.24 to 35: mostly sound, but some scores lack evidence, owners are committees or vague, triggers are missing, or the money arithmetic is off.0 to 23: rows missing, risks copied without scoring, no owners, or no use of the measures.
2. Feedback record (full coach conversation, what changed and why)1514 to 15: full conversation included; changes clearly tied to specific feedback, with reasons for anything kept or rejected.8 to 13: conversation included but changes are vague or not tied to feedback.0 to 7: conversation missing, cut down to a summary, or no revision.
3. Ethics exercise (ACHE Code, Section VI)1514 to 15: each of the six AI steps gets a sound call with a reason, and the unsafe ones are replaced; the two board sentences are honest about the phishing result and the missing drill, with a plan and date; the Section VI answer explains reasonable grounds, raising it inside Kestrel Falls first (Doug, then Laura or compliance), and when a report to ACHE would be required.8 to 13: most steps handled, but a dishonest step is kept, or the Section VI answer is loose.0 to 7: parts missing, the AI draft is accepted as is, or no link to the Code.
4. Copilot use with verification (Step 4)1514 to 15: your own prompt clearly gives Copilot permission to say it doesn't know (for example, to mark unsupported scores as uncertain); you check every row against the files, name any invented facts or numbers, judge whether Copilot's 'uncertain' marks were right, and explain where you disagreed.8 to 13: permission is vague, or checking covers only some rows.0 to 7: no permission to express uncertainty, no checking, or a copied prompt.
5. AI-use disclosure55: names the tools, the parts they helped with, and how you checked.2 to 4: incomplete.0 to 1: missing.
6. Reflection109 to 10: compares Step 3 with the final register, names specific changes and why, says what you learned from letting Copilot say 'I don't know,' and connects to a real case of ranking hard-to-compare risks as a healthcare manager.5 to 8: general reflection.0 to 4: missing or off topic.

Not graded on its own: Step 3 (first call without AI). It is your starting point for the reflection. There is no single right register; scores are judgments, and the evidence and reasons are graded.
