Module 6 rubric: Step 3, Big Bang or Phased

100 points. Your work is graded on reasoning, not on matching one right answer. Copy this whole page into your coach chat in Step 6.

Reasoning and judgment (Step 5 comparison, memo, and safeguard plan)4036 to 40: every cell of the comparison is filled from the files; the options are compared on the same criteria; the recommendation follows from the facts and states its trade-offs openly, with the numbers that matter (for example, Extended Support for 2029 in Option B, temporary interfaces, support helpers, fewer appointments); the plan deals with the sites most at risk (Walk-in Care, the rural clinics, the ambulance service, staff who work in both a clinic and the hospital); the safeguard plan says what happens, who does it, when, and how you will know it works; the "what would change my mind" fact is checkable.24 to 35: a sound pick, but some trade-offs or at-risk sites are missed, the numbers are not used, or the safeguard plan is general.0 to 23: the pick ignores the dates or costs, uses facts not in the files, or parts are missing.

Ethics exercise (ACHE Code, Section V)1514 to 15: each of the six recommendations gets a sound call with a reason, and the draft is corrected where it is wrong; the community notice plan names who outside the hospital is told, when, and how, including patients who prefer another language; the note to Nate ties clearly to Section V items 1, 5, and 6.8 to 13: most items handled, but an access or partner problem is accepted, or the link to the Code is loose.0 to 7: parts missing, the draft is accepted as is, or no link to the Code.
