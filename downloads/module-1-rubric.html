Module 1 rubric: Healthcare IS Foundations

100 points. Your work is graded on reasoning, not on matching one right answer. Copy this whole page into your coach chat in Step 6.

CriterionPointsFull marksPartialLow

Reasoning and judgment (Part 1 inventory and value map, Part 2 memo)4036 to 40: every system has specific value (named areas and how) and a realistic one-day downtime impact for a 300-bed hospital with clinics and ambulances. The memo picks three systems with clear reasons, trade-offs, and who is affected.24 to 35: most systems covered; some value or impact statements are generic, or the memo's reasons are thin.0 to 23: missing systems, generic or incorrect statements, or the memo gives no reasons.

Ethics exercise (ACHE Code, Section V)1514 to 15: names who is left out and how, two workable options with honest trade-offs, and a recommendation clearly tied to items 5 and 6.8 to 13: covers the parts but links to the Code are loose or trade-offs are missing.0 to 7: parts missing or no link to the Code.
