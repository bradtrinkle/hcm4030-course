Module 8 rubric: Step 5, Customization

100 points. Your work is graded on reasoning, not on matching one right answer. Copy this whole page into your coach chat in Step 6.

CriterionPointsFull marksPartialLow
1. Reasoning and judgment (Step 5 decision log, summary, reply)4036 to 40: every request has a decision and a reason that fits what the new EHR offers as standard (configure before customize; custom work only for safety, legal need, or a clear benefit); five-year costs are right; the approved total is shown with arithmetic and is at or under $2.4 million, with what was cut or deferred named; each AI feature has a sound decision and specific before-and-after conditions (local validation, human review, monitoring, patient notice); the reply is honest and offers a better way to meet the need.24 to 35: mostly sound, but some costs are wrong, the total is not checked against the reserve, or AI conditions are general.0 to 23: approves everything or nothing without reasons, ignores the reserve, or parts are missing.
2. Feedback record (full coach conversation, what changed and why)1514 to 15: full conversation included; changes clearly tied to specific feedback, with reasons for anything kept or rejected.8 to 13: conversation included but changes are vague or not tied to feedback.0 to 7: conversation missing, cut down to a summary, or no revision.
3. Ethics exercise (ACHE Code, Section III)1514 to 15: each of the six rules gets a sound call with a reason, and the AI draft is corrected where it is wrong; the oversight rule says who approves, what is checked before go-live, what is monitored after, and who can turn a feature off; the note to Doug ties clearly to Section III item 14.8 to 13: most rules handled, but an unsafe rule is kept, or the link to the Code is loose.0 to 7: parts missing, the AI draft is accepted as is, or no link to the Code.
4. Copilot use with verification (Step 4)1514 to 15: your own few-shot prompt gives two clear worked examples (ideally different decisions) and the decision choices; you check every row's cost and reason against the file, name the rows you would change, and say whether Copilot copied your examples too closely.8 to 13: examples are vague, or checking covers only some rows.0 to 7: no examples, no checking, or a copied prompt.
5. AI-use disclosure55: names the tools, the parts they helped with, and how you checked.2 to 4: incomplete.0 to 1: missing.
6. Reflection109 to 10: compares Step 3 with the final log, names specific changes and why, says what you learned about examples steering AI, and connects to a real buy, build, or go-without decision a healthcare manager might make.5 to 8: general reflection.0 to 4: missing or off topic.

Not graded on its own: Step 3 (first call without AI). It is your starting point for the reflection. Reply choices A, B, and C in Step 5 are graded the same way. There is no single right log; reasons and arithmetic are graded.
