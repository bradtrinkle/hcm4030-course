Your story: your first job at Kestrel Falls

For this term you play yourself in a made-up job. Kestrel Falls Community Hospital is a fictional, independent, not-for-profit hospital in the fictional town of Kestrel Falls, Iowa. It has about 300 beds, 10 outpatient clinics, and its own ambulance service. You are the new Assistant to the Chief of Staff.

In your first week you learn the hospital's information systems. Then the news arrives: the company behind the hospital's electronic health record (EHR) will stop supporting it. Over the term you help the hospital choose a new EHR and move to it, step by step, the way real hospitals do. You finish by presenting your recommendation to the hospital's board.

What this means for you each week. Someone at Kestrel Falls brings you a realistic problem with fictional data. You learn the ideas, make your own first call without AI, practice with Microsoft Copilot, build a deliverable a healthcare leader would use, get feedback from your AI coach and revise, work through an ethics question from the ACHE Code of Ethics, and reflect on what you learned. You are graded on your reasoning, not on finding one right answer.

Meet three people you will hear from often

Laura Hemmingsen

Chief of Staff, your boss and mentor. She uses AI every day and expects you to check it every time.

Doug Thielen

Chief Information Officer. Knows every system in the building and what it costs to run.

Dr. Richard Halvorsen

Surgeon and skeptic. Needs proof before he trusts a new system, so build your case as if he will read it.

Every person, the hospital, the town, every vendor, and every number in this course is fictional. Character videos use AI-generated avatars. The skills you build are real.

How every module works

1 · The problem lands

A message from someone at Kestrel Falls sets up the week.

2 · Learn

A video lecture (with a transcript) and reading, with practice questions.

3 · Your first call, without AI

A quick first take in your own words, before Copilot, so you can check its work.

4 · Practice with Copilot

Write your own prompts and check what Copilot gives you.

5 · Build the deliverable

The work product a healthcare leader would actually use.

6 · Coach feedback and revision

Your AI coach gives feedback, never answers. You improve your work.

7 · Ethics exercise

A hands-on case from the ACHE Code of Ethics.

8 · Reflection

What you learned and what you would do differently.

About 6 hours a week

Two-week modules take about 12 hours. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer and keep automatic saving on.
