Start Here

Do these five things before you open Module 1. They take about 50 minutes, and that time is part of Week 1's 6 hours. Tick each box as you finish it; your ticks save in this browser.

1 · Watch the welcome video · 5 min

Welcome video from your instructor
The video will appear here. If it does not, tell your instructor and keep going.

The lecture videos in this course are presented by your instructor or by an AI-generated avatar of your instructor; each video is labeled.

2 · Read the syllabus and tour Canvas · 15 min

  1. Open the course in Canvas and read the syllabus. Pay special attention to: the required Microsoft 365 plan, the grading weights, no late work, and the redo rules.
  2. Find where you upload each module's submission in Canvas. This site builds your Word file; Canvas is where you turn it in.
Redos in one line. You may redo any assignment up to 3 times, within 14 days after its grade posts, and all redos by Thursday, May 13. Your highest attempt counts. A redo is only for work you first turned in on time.

3 · Get Microsoft 365 Personal (student offer) · 15 min

All AI work in this course uses Microsoft Copilot in a Microsoft 365 Personal plan, which includes Copilot in Word, Excel, and PowerPoint. Microsoft offers college students a discount (about $4.99 a month; check the current price on Microsoft's page). You do not use the university-provided Copilot Chat for course work.

  1. Go to Microsoft's college student pricing page and follow its steps. You will verify that you are a student.
  2. If you already have Microsoft 365 Personal, you can skip the purchase. If you use a Microsoft 365 Family plan, you must be the plan's owner, because Microsoft says AI features are only for the owner.
  3. The plan renews every month until you cancel it. You may cancel after final grades are posted.
  4. Save your course files in OneDrive with AutoSave on. Copilot in Excel needs it.

Instructor note (draft): exact sign-in and "where to find Copilot" steps will be added here after the tool test on the student plan.

4 · Set up your AI coach · 15 min

Every module has a coach step. Your coach is Copilot following the instructions below. It gives you feedback, never answers. You will save the full coach conversation and turn it in: it is your feedback record and is worth 15 of 100 points in every module.

  1. Click Copy the coach instructions, or download them and keep the file in OneDrive.
    Download the coach instructions
  2. Open Copilot, start a new chat, paste the instructions, and send. Then type: "This is a test. Ask me one question about my goals for this course."
  3. Check that the coach asks you a question instead of doing work for you.
See the coach instructions
HCM 4030 AI COACH INSTRUCTIONS (Paste this at the start of every new coach conversation in Microsoft Copilot. Then paste your draft.) You are my feedback coach for HCM 4030, Healthcare Information Systems, at Iowa State University. The course is set at Kestrel Falls Community Hospital, a fictional 300-bed independent, not-for-profit hospital in Iowa with 10 outpatient clinics and an ambulance service. All people, organizations, and data in the course are fictional. Your job is to give feedback, never answers. - Do not write, rewrite, or complete any part of my work. Do not give me sample answers, model paragraphs, or finished tables. - Ask me questions that help me see gaps in my reasoning. Point to the place in my draft where each issue is. - Score my draft against the rubric I paste, criterion by criterion, and explain each score in one or two sentences. - For each criterion that is not at full marks, tell me what is missing or weak, and ask one question that would help me improve it. - Check whether I explained why, not just what. Healthcare leaders need reasons, trade-offs, and who is affected. - Check whether I verified what AI (Copilot) told me, and whether my AI-use disclosure is complete. - If I ask you to just write it for me, remind me that you give feedback only. - If my draft contains anything that looks like real patient, employee, or organization data, stop and tell me to remove it. When I paste a revised draft, tell me what improved and what still needs work. Keep your feedback short and specific: a list, not an essay.
